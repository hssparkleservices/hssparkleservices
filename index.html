Frequently asked

Good to know before you book.

How long does carpet take to dry?

Most carpets are dry within 2–4 hours using our hot water extraction process, depending on room ventilation and carpet type. We'll advise on the day and can recommend keeping windows open or fans running to speed things up.

Do I need to move furniture beforehand?

We move light furniture as standard — sofas, chairs, coffee tables. For heavier or fitted items, we'll clean around them or ask you to move them in advance where possible.

Are your products safe for children and pets?

Yes. We use effective, low-toxicity products that are safe once the treated area is dry, and we're always happy to talk through anything specific to your home.

Can you remove stains and odours?

Most everyday stains and odours — food, drink, pet-related — lift during the standard clean. Stubborn or set-in stains are assessed on-site and quoted honestly, rather than promised and not delivered.

Do you provide paperwork for end-of-tenancy or landlord records?

Yes — we provide an invoice and, on request, written confirmation of the clean carried out, useful for deposit and checkout purposes.

How do I pay?

Card, bank transfer or cash on the day — whichever is easiest for you. Payment is only taken once the job is complete.
